What is a deepfake?

A deepfake is an image or video where a face, voice, or motion has been generated or altered by an AI model, usually convincingly enough to pass for real. The same generative techniques behind style filters and voice cloning can be pushed further to fabricate someone saying or doing something that never happened.

DeepDetect is a college project built to explore the other side of that problem: given a piece of media, can a model learn to notice the artifacts a generation process leaves behind, and say so with a measurable confidence?

Why detection matters

  • Misinformation: fabricated footage can spread and get screenshotted long before a correction catches up.
  • Identity misuse: a person's face or voice can be reused in content they never took part in.
  • Fraud: synthetic media is increasingly used to slip past identity checks and video calls.
  • Social media manipulation: a convincingly edited clip can shape opinion before anyone verifies it.
  • Digital trust: every unverified clip in circulation makes it a little harder to trust what a camera shows.

  4. 4
